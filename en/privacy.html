2. Hosting and server log files

The website is hosted by STRATO in Berlin, Germany. When you open a page, the server processes technically necessary data: IP address, date and time, the page requested, amount of data transferred, browser and operating system, and possibly the page you came from.

This is needed to deliver the website and run it securely. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. I don't analyse this data or combine it with other data.

3. Contact by email

If you write to me, I process your email address and the content of your message in order to reply. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if it concerns a possible collaboration, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. I delete the message once it has been dealt with and no statutory retention obligations apply.

4. Light or dark mode setting

If you press the button for light or dark mode, your browser stores this choice locally (local storage, entry “theme”). The information never leaves your browser and is not sent to me. Nothing is stored without a click. Storing it is strictly necessary for this feature you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete the entry at any time in your browser settings.

5. Links to other websites

This website links to LinkedIn and to SiteLock's verification page. Data is only sent to these providers once you click the link. From then on, their privacy policies apply.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21). Just send me an email.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the Thuringian State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.
